Our Journey

From a Small Office in Colombo to Powering 30+ Businesses

We are solutions provider for Retail and Restaurant business verticals. Our solutions enables the business owners and the management to monitor sales, staff performance, manage inventory and monitor costing. Magnex Solutions is a channel focused company with many channel partners worldwide and establishing new channel partners. Launched in 2017 our company is rapidly growing in installation base. We keep exploring the possibilities or providing latest technologies with great features at an affordable cost. We understand that our client success is our success.
